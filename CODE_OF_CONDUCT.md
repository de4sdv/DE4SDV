# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Assume good intent, but accept responsibility for impact.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully.
- Credit contributions of all types.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without consent
- Inappropriate sexual attention
- Any conduct that makes participation unsafe

## Reporting

Report concerns to the maintainers by opening a private security/contact channel once configured.

Until a formal committee exists, project founders handle reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants from project spaces depending on severity and context.
