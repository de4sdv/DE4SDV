- **Source:** `closed_loop_driving_operational_context.sysml`
- **Viewpoint:** `closedLoopDrivingContextViewpoint` (`OperationalContextDefinitionViewpoint`)
- **Concern:** `closedLoopDrivingContextConcern`
- **Exposes:** `closedLoopDrivingOperationalContext`, `closedLoopDrivingOperationalContext::*`, `closedLoopDrivingOperation`
- **Depth:** `2`
- **Render:** `asTreeDiagram`

- **Diagram status:** Published from the committed SysIDE SVG.
- **Presentation note:** SysIDE lists the actions of `closedLoopDrivingOperation` but does not draw the flows between them. The flows are modeled in the source, in the action definition `ClosedLoopDrivingOperation`: camera images and ego motion from the vehicle into planning, steering and acceleration commands to the vehicle, and the published plan, vehicle state and health to AAOS SDV, with no flow back into the loop.
