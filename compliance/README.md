# Compliance

Status: **incubating**.

This area contains draft structures for safety, security, regulatory, and
homologation-related evidence. Templates are starting points, not completed
analyses, verification evidence, certification, or authority approval.

An active compliance increment must identify its System 1 subject, assurance
concern, source provenance, qualified reviewer, evidence maturity, and open
limitations. Unfilled templates remain examples and must not be cited as
project evidence.
